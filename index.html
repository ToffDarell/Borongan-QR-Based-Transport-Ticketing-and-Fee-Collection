QR-Based Ticketing for Borongan City

The Borongan Transport QR Ticketing System is a modern solution designed to streamline public transportation in Borongan City. Passengers can easily pay fares using QR codes, while drivers and operators benefit from real-time tracking and automated fee collection.

Built for tricycles, jeepneys, multicabs, and buses this system ensures transparency, efficiency, and convenience for both drivers and commuters.

About

QR-Based Transport Fee Collection

The QR-Based Transport Fee Collection System is a web-based application developed to improve the collection and monitoring of transport fees for public utility vehicles operating in Borongan City.

The system allows drivers of tricycles, jeepneys, multicabs, and buses to register their information and receive a unique QR code. During fee collection, the QR code is scanned to automatically identify the driver, display the correct transport fee based on the vehicle type, record the payment, and print an official receipt.

The system helps reduce manual recording, improve transparency, and provide accurate reports for transport fee collection.
